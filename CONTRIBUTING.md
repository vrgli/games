# Contributing

I build, you fork. These games are free to copy, change and publish as your own.

- **Fork a game.** Each game has its own repo (see the list in the [README](README.md)). Click **Fork**, clone it and open `game/index.html` in a browser. No build step.
- **Licence.** MIT plus one condition: keep a visible "A VRG game" credit linking to https://play.vrg.li. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
- **Ideas and bugs.** Open an issue on the game's repo, or on this one for the Play site itself. Small, specific reports are the easiest to act on.
- **Pull requests.** Welcome for fixes and small improvements. Keep a change to one thing, and say what you played to check it. A change that turns a game into a different game is better as your own fork.
- **Run the Play site.** `npx wrangler dev` (needs a Cloudflare account for the Durable Objects).
